Privacy Policy

App: ZeroLedger Package: com.zeroledger.expense Effective Date: November 22, 2026

ZeroLedger (“we”, “our”, or “the App”) is an offline-first personal financial ledger and budget planner. We believe that your personal financial records are strictly your private business. This Privacy Policy discloses our practices concerning data collection, processing, storage, and permissions.

1. Zero-Internet Architecture & Core Privacy Guarantee

🛡️ ZeroLedger does not possess internet access. The application does not declare, request, or use the android.permission.INTERNET permission in its Android Manifest.

Because there is no network connection, it is physically and technically impossible for ZeroLedger to transmit, upload, sell, or share your financial records, SMS messages, account numbers, or personal data to any external server, third party, or cloud service. 100% of your data remains strictly on your physical device.

2. Sensitive Permissions & How They Are Used

ZeroLedger requests only the permissions strictly required to deliver core personal finance features:

A. SMS Permissions (android.permission.READ_SMS, android.permission.RECEIVE_SMS)

  • Purpose: To automatically detect, categorize, and record incoming transactional SMS alerts sent by financial institutions (banks, credit card issuers, and UPI providers).
  • Core Functionality Exception: ZeroLedger uses SMS permission under Google Play’s permitted “SMS-based financial transactions / SMS-based money management” exception.
  • On-Device Processing: SMS parsing is conducted 100% locally using on-device regular expression algorithms.
  • Strict Data Minimization: The App strictly filters for transactional keywords and institutional bank sender IDs. Personal conversations, private text messages, and One-Time Passwords (OTPs) are completely ignored and discarded. They are never stored, logged, or recorded.
  • Prominent Disclosure: A clear, in-app prominent disclosure is presented to the user prior to requesting the Android OS runtime SMS permission.

B. Biometric Authentication (android.permission.USE_BIOMETRIC, android.permission.USE_FINGERPRINT)

  • Purpose: To provide an optional biometric app lock (fingerprint or facial unlock) to secure your ledger against unauthorized access on your device.
  • Security: Handled exclusively by the Android operating system’s native BiometricPrompt API. ZeroLedger never accesses, collects, or stores your raw biometric data or fingerprints.

C. Notifications (android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS)

  • Purpose: To alert you locally when a budget envelope is nearing its monthly limit, or when an incoming transaction requires manual review. Notifications are generated entirely on-device.

3. Financial Services & Personal Loans Policy

In strict compliance with Google Play’s Financial Services Policy:

  • ZeroLedger does NOT offer, broker, originate, facilitate, or service personal loans, microloans, payday loans, or lines of credit.
  • The App includes a personal bookkeeping feature allowing users to tag expenses as “Lent / Hand Loan” or “Reimbursed”. This feature is solely an offline personal accounting tool (for tracking split bills or money given to friends/family) and does not involve commercial lending, credit checks, or financial intermediation.

4. Third-Party Services, Analytics & Advertisements

  • No Analytics SDKs: We do not embed Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, or any tracking libraries.
  • No Ad Networks: ZeroLedger contains zero advertisements. We do not integrate Google AdMob or any ad networks.
  • No Third-Party Sharing: We do not sell, rent, trade, or share user data with any third party, data broker, or marketing entity.

5. Data Storage, Retention & Deletion

  • Local Storage: All transaction records, budget envelopes, account nicknames, and user preferences are stored exclusively in an encrypted, sandboxed local SQLite database on your device.
  • User Control & Deletion: You maintain full ownership and control of your data at all times. You can delete individual records inside the App, clear all data via Android Settings > Apps > ZeroLedger > Storage > Clear Data, or permanently remove all data by uninstalling the App.

6. Children’s Privacy

ZeroLedger is intended for general audiences managing personal finances and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
